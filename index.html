// Our Story

Coffee as
philosophy.

KURO began with a simple obsession — finding the world's most extraordinary coffees and presenting them with the reverence they deserve. We believe a great cup of coffee is not an accident. It is the result of intention at every step: soil, harvest, roast, and pour.

Our roastery in Edinburgh works with farmers directly — paying above fair trade prices and visiting each farm personally. This is how we ensure the cup in your hands carries a story worth tasting.
