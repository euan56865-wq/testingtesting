Our Story

A Wee Gem in the
Heart of Killie

The Auld Barrel has been a beloved fixture on Bank Street since 1987. Owned and run by the MacAllister family for three generations, we've kept the spirit of a true Scottish local alive — real ales, great whisky, and a welcome for everyone.

Whether you're stopping in after a match, celebrating a birthday, or simply after a quiet pint, you'll always find a familiar face and a comfortable seat.

🥃
Over 80 Whiskies From local Ayrshire drams to rare Speyside single malts — our whisky selection is second to none in Kilmarnock.
🍺
Rotating Real Ales Six cask ales always on, rotating seasonally. We champion Scottish independent breweries.
